# Please try this: Lillyjean v0.3.0

Thank you for testing Lillyjean! This first test release is about one thing: **sign up and track what you
read**. It takes about 15 minutes. Use your phone if you can, and add the app to your home screen first
(Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen; Chrome: ⋮ → Add to Home screen).

Something odd, confusing or missing? Tell us in the app: **avatar (top right) → Settings → Send feedback**.
Short notes are perfect, for example "step 4: I expected …".

## 1. Create your account

1. Open the link you received and tap **Create account**.
2. Enter your email and a password of at least 12 characters, then type the 6-digit code from the email.
3. Choose a username for your library card.
4. **What are you reading now?** Search for a book you are reading and tap it, so it gets a check mark.
   You can choose more than one, or tap **Skip**.
5. Set a reading pledge for this year (or choose "No goal, just reading") and tap **Finish**.

You should see Home with your books under **Currently reading**.

## 2. Find and add books

1. Tap the **+** at the bottom, then **Add book**.
2. Search for a title, an author or an ISBN. Try the **Title** and **Author** filters.
3. Tap **+ Want to read** next to a book you would like to read one day.
4. Can't find a book? Tap **Add it manually**: title and author are enough.

## 3. Open a book

1. Tap a book to open its page: cover, description, and the Google rating if there is one.
2. Tap **View on Goodreads ↗** if you want to compare.
3. Under **My status**, choose Want to read, Reading or Read. When you are reading, tap **Edit** to note how
   far you are (optional).

## 4. Swipe to change a status

1. On Home, slide a book a little to the **left**: three buttons appear. Tap one.
2. Slide a book **far** to the left and let go: it moves to the next status.
3. Changed your mind? Tap **Undo** in the message at the bottom (you have 5 seconds).
4. Prefer tapping? Tap the status label on the right of a book.

## 5. Finish a book

1. Tap **+** → **Mark as read**, and pick a book you are reading.
2. Pick the day you finished it (today is filled in) and tap **Mark as read**.
3. Home now counts it under **This year**, compared with your pledge.
4. Read a favourite again? Open the book → **⋯** → **Mark as read again**. It counts again.

## 6. Tell us what you think

- What felt easy, and where did you hesitate?
- Did swiping feel natural, or did you rather tap?
- Did you find the books you looked for? Which ones were missing?

Send it through **Settings → Send feedback**. Thank you!

## Good to know

- Readlists, challenges and friends come in the next releases: see **Settings → What's coming**.
- Book data comes from Open Library and Google Books, so covers and descriptions can differ per book.
