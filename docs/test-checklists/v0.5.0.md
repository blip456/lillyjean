# Please try this: Lillyjean v0.5.0

Thank you for testing again! This release is about **challenges**: set yourself a reading challenge, pick a
book that fits, read it, and collect a stamp. It takes about 15 minutes. Use your phone if you can.

Something odd, confusing or missing? Tell us in the app: **avatar (top right) → Settings → Send feedback**.
Short notes are perfect, for example "step 2: I expected …".

## 1. Make a challenge

1. Tap **+** at the bottom, then **New challenge**.
2. Give it a title, for example a theme you like, and describe what a book needs to fit it. The start date is
   today; you can choose an end date if you want one.
3. Add at least **5 books** that fit: tap **+ Add books** and search, or **Import from a readlist**. The
   counter shows how many you have; **Create challenge** turns on at 5.
4. Tap **Create challenge**. You land on your new challenge.

## 2. Pick your book

1. On the challenge, tap **Add this book** next to the book you want to read. It becomes **Your pick**.
2. Changed your mind? Tap **Add this book** on another one: it asks first.
3. Want a book that is not on the list? Tap **Submit my own pick**, search for it and tap **Use as my pick**.
   Own picks are accepted right away.

## 3. Read it

1. Your pick now shows "Part of 1 challenge" on Home and in the search, and the book page shows
   **Fits your challenges**.
2. When you finish it, mark it as read (swipe it, or open the book). The sheet says **This completes 1
   challenge**. Save it: a stamp lands and you see **Challenge completed!**
3. Only books finished on or after the start date count. Reading a book again counts too.
4. Tapped by mistake? Close the celebration and tap **Undo**: the challenge is open again.

## 4. Look around

1. Tap **Challenges** in the bar at the bottom: **Active** shows your running and upcoming challenges,
   **Past** your completed ones, each with a DONE stamp.
2. On Home: your completed challenges next to the books you read this year, and **Ongoing challenges**.
3. Your profile: the library card counts your challenges and shows a stamp for each one.
4. On a challenge, **⋯** lets you edit it or delete it. After the start you can still change the dates and
   the books; the title and theme stay as they are. Completed challenges can't be deleted.

## 5. Tell us what you think

- Was choosing 5 books too much work, or just right?
- Did the celebration feel good? Too much, too little?
- What kind of challenges did you make?

Send it through **Settings → Send feedback**. Thank you!

## Good to know

- Challenges with friends and official Lillyjean challenges come in the next releases: see **Settings →
  What's coming**.
- A challenge that ends without a finished book simply disappears. Only completed ones stay under Past.
