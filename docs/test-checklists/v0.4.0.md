# Please try this: Lillyjean v0.4.0

Thank you for testing again! This release is about **readlists**: collect books in your own lists, like
playlists. Covers should also load faster now. It takes about 10 minutes. Use your phone if you can.

Something odd, confusing or missing? Tell us in the app: **avatar (top right) → Settings → Send feedback**.
Short notes are perfect, for example "step 3: I expected …".

## 1. Make a readlist

1. Tap **Readlists** in the bar at the bottom. You see your three book lists (Want to read, Reading, Read)
   and "Make your first readlist".
2. Tap **+** at the bottom, then **New readlist**.
3. Give it a name, and a description if you like, then tap **Create**. You land in your new, empty
   readlist.

## 2. Add books to it

1. Go to Home and **press and hold** a book for about half a second. A menu opens.
2. Tap **Add to readlist…** and tick your readlist. Ticks are saved straight away. Tap **Done**.
3. Open a book's page: tap **+ Add to readlist** under My status. You can tick more than one readlist,
   or make a new one right there with **New readlist**.
4. At the bottom of the book's page, **In your readlists** shows where the book is. Tap one to open it.

## 3. Look at your readlist

1. Open your readlist: the newest book is on top, and the first four covers make the picture at the top.
2. Hold a book in the readlist and tap **Remove from this readlist**. Changed your mind? Tap **Undo**.
3. Tap **⋯ → Edit** to rename it, or **⋯ → Delete** to delete it. Deleting asks first, and your books
   stay in your Want to read, Reading and Read lists.

## 4. Home

1. On Home, scroll down to **Your readlists**: your lists side by side.
2. **All** opens the Readlists page, and **+ New** makes another readlist.
