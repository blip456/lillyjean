## Open questions

### N · Navigation

- **N-PO1** Alt 1 (classic tab bar), Alt 2 (bookshelf spines + Quick log) or a hybrid of both?
  > A hybrid option does sound intresting
- **N-PO2** Profile behind the avatar (as drawn) or as its own tab (dropping the center action)?
  > Profile behind the avatar
- **N-PO3** Which actions belong in the global + / Quick log menu, and in which order?
  > 1. Add Book, 2. Mark as read, 3. New Challenge, 4. New List
- **N-PO4** Do we need a notifications inbox (bell) in v1, or are email/push notifications plus the activity feed enough?
  > No
- **N-U1** Which navigation style feels more natural to you, and why?
  > 1 or 2. It looks easy and simple to navigate. Quick accessible
- **N-U2** When you open the app, what is the first thing you would want to do?

### Where Jean agrees with the Product Owner

Profile behind the avatar, no notifications inbox, email + password or email code sign-in, adjustable
yearly goal, re-reads count, manual book entry in v1, members join challenges themselves, own picks
accepted automatically, no late completion, one book can complete several challenges, books must be
finished after the challenge start, club members see all reading statuses, swipe to change a book's
status, no pick-my-next-read deck, phone-first usage.
